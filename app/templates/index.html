Welcome to KidneyProtect

We leverage advanced machine learning models to provide reliable predictive diagnostics for Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD). By analyzing physiological parameters, our platform helps in the early detection and management of kidney health.

What is CKD?

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) means your kidneys are damaged and can't filter blood the way they should. The disease is called "chronic" because the damage to your kidneys happens slowly over a long period of time.

Early detection is vital because kidney damage usually cannot be reversed. However, identifying it early allows for treatments that can slow the disease progression.

Common Risk Factors

Our AI model analyzes these critical health factors:

  • Diabetes Mellitus & High Blood Sugar
  • Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)
  • Hemoglobin & Red Blood Cell counts
  • Albumin levels and Pus cell evaluation
